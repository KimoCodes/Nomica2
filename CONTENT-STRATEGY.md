# Content Strategy

## Exercise Library
- **250+ exercises** across 9 muscle groups
- Each exercise includes: name, muscle group, difficulty (1-3), instructions, video URL placeholder
- Difficulty levels: 1 = Beginner, 2 = Intermediate, 3 = Advanced

## Workout Templates
- **Beginner**: 3-day split, 30-45 min sessions, 3 sets × 10-12 reps
- **Intermediate**: 4-day split, 45-60 min sessions, 4 sets × 8-12 reps
- **Advanced**: 5-6 day split, 60-90 min sessions, 4-5 sets × 6-12 reps

## Nutrition Content
- **USA-primary** food database with nutrition data
- **Global** food items for international users
- Meal logging with macros tracking (protein, carbs, fat)
- Food substitution suggestions

## Educational Content
- Exercise form instructions embedded in workout flow
- RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion) guidance
- Recovery recommendations based on sleep, stress, energy
- Progressive overload principles explained

## Gamification Content
- 18+ badges across 6 categories (workout, streak, milestone, social, checkin, special)
- Points system: workout completion, consistency streaks, milestones
- Achievement unlocking with visual feedback

## Notifications & Engagement
- Daily motivational notifications (configurable)
- Workout reminders
- Streak milestones celebration
- At-risk user re-engagement campaigns

## Localization
- English (US) as primary language
- Date format: MM/DD/YYYY
- Units: Imperial (lbs, inches) with metric conversion
- Currency: USD
